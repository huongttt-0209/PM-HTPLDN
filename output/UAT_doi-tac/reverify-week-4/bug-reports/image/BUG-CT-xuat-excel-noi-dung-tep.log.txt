[6] LECH NGAY — doi chieu man hinh vs tep (phat hien ngoai phieu)
    Cot 'Thoi gian bat dau' / 'Thoi gian ket thuc' trong tep lech 1 NGAY VE TRUOC
    so voi gia tri hien tren bang danh sach:

      Ma CT              | Man hinh hien thi        | Trong tep Excel
      -------------------+--------------------------+----------------------------------
      CT-20260724-0001   | 1/1/2026 - 31/12/2026    | 2025-12-31 17:00 / 2026-12-30 17:00

    Du lieu goc phia may chu tra ve cho CT-20260725-0002:
      thoiGianBatDau = "2025-12-31T17:00:00.000Z"
      2025-12-31 17:00 UTC + 7 gio = 01/01/2026 00:00 gio Viet Nam  -> man hinh quy doi DUNG
    => Du lieu luu dung, man hinh quy doi dung, chi khau ghi ra tep KHONG quy doi mui gio.
